NORRVIND · Friends Edition

NorrNote21FE

Your notes on Android and Windows. Create notes, lists and reminders, attach documents and images, and optionally synchronize them through your own Google Drive account.

Free, non-commercial Friends Edition · Public release in preparation

Designed for two devices

NorrNote21FE keeps local data on your phone or PC so you can use notes even when offline. When you connect the same Google account on both devices, the app synchronizes notes and attachments using Google Drive API.

Features

  • Text notes and checklists
  • Reminders and home-screen / desktop widgets
  • Image and document attachments
  • Send a note as text or as a ZIP archive containing its attachments
  • Optional cross-device synchronization via Google Drive

Google account access

Google Drive access is optional for local use. When you choose to sign in, NorrNote21FE requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission to create and access files used by the application within your Google Drive. The app uses a dedicated folder named NorrNote21FE. It does not need permission to browse your entire Drive.

Availability

The Friends Edition is undergoing compatibility and sign-in testing. Public installer files will appear in the project's official GitHub Releases after validation. The personal NorrNote21 and the Friends Edition are separate applications.
