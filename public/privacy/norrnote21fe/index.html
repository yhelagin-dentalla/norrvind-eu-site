Privacy Policy

How the NorrNote21FE application for Android and Windows handles your information.

Effective date: 10 October 2026

1. Who we are

NorrNote21FE is a free, non-commercial notes application developed by the NORRVIND team. Contact: support@norrvind.app.

2. Data you create

The application stores notes, checklists, reminders, settings and attachments on your device. Some metadata (including modification times and identifiers) is used to keep notes synchronized. The application does not require a NORRVIND account.

3. Google OAuth and Google Drive

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app uses Google OAuth to obtain authorization and requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This permits the app to create and work with files associated with the application, rather than granting general access to all files in your Google Drive.

The app creates a dedicated folder named NorrNote21FE and maintains synchronization data such as database-personal.json and attachment blobs. This can include note text, file contents and associated metadata. The content is stored in the Google account you connect and transferred directly using Google's APIs. Google account identifiers and authorization tokens are used locally as needed to maintain the connection. We do not ask for or store your Google password.

NORRVIND does not operate a separate server that collects or stores the contents of your synchronized notes. Google's handling of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Other permissions

On Android, the app may request notifications and access to files you select. On Windows, the app can use notifications and files selected by you. Sharing a note or file with another app is initiated by you and is subject to the recipient app's privacy policy.

5. Data sharing and advertising

We do not sell your personal data or use your notes for advertising. Your selected external sharing target or connected Google Drive may process data as part of the feature you request.

6. Retention and deletion

Local notes persist until you remove them or uninstall the application, subject to operating-system backups and local storage settings. You can disconnect the Google account through the app; disconnecting does not automatically erase files already stored in Google Drive. To remove cloud copies, delete the dedicated NorrNote21FE folder and its contents using Google Drive. For help, contact support@norrvind.app.

7. Security

Google API connections use HTTPS and account authorization. The app is not described as end-to-end encrypted; do not assume cloud files are accessible only to you under all circumstances. Protect your device and Google account and maintain independent backups of important data.

8. Children and updates

This is a general productivity tool and is not specifically directed at children. We may update this policy when functionality changes and publish the new effective date here.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, access requests or deletion guidance: support@norrvind.app.
