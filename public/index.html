How we build

Software should feel like a good tool.

Its purpose should be obvious. Important controls should be visible. It should help you work without forcing you into an account, a platform or a stream of subscriptions.

UnderstandablePredictableLocal when practicalUser controlled
NORRVIND principle

Less interface.
More useful work.

Local first

Core work should remain available even without a permanent connection or a mandatory NORRVIND account.

Visible controls

Important actions stay explicit instead of disappearing behind opaque automation.

Focused products

Each app starts with a concrete job rather than a desire to become a platform.

Calm design

Colour and motion are used to clarify, not to compete for attention.

Simple ownership

Where a product can sensibly be owned instead of rented, we prefer the simpler model.
